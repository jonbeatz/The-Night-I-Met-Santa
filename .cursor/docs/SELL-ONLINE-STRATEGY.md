## 3. Critical format constraints (this exact book)

| Fact | Why it matters |
|------|----------------|
| **Amazon KDP has no 8.5×8.5″ hardcover** | Your gift HC cannot be reprinted *as HC* on KDP |
| **KDP hardcover also needs 75–550 pages** | Your ~34 pp book wouldn’t qualify for KDP HC anyway |
| **KDP *does* support 8.5×8.5″ paperback** | Amazon physical = same square trim — **no art reformat** |

## 4. ISBN and barcode (what they actually are)

### ISBN
- Unique ID for **one publisher + one title + one format** (HC ≠ paperback ≠ ebook).  
- US agency: **Bowker** → [myidentifiers.com](https://www.myidentifiers.com)  
- Ballpark: **~$125** for 1 · **~$295** for 10 (confirm at checkout).

| Source | Who is “publisher”? | Portable to other platforms? |
|--------|---------------------|------------------------------|
| **Bowker (you buy)** | Your imprint | **Yes** |
| Free Lulu ISBN | Lulu.com | **No** |
| Free KDP ISBN | Independently Published / Amazon | **No** |

**Recommendation (Plan B-soft):** Buy a **10-pack**, create an imprint (e.g. “Farrell Books” / studio name), assign:
- ISBN-1 → **Paperback** (commercial softcover — first sell SKU)  
- ISBN-2 → **Ebook** (optional; Kindle also gets an ASIN)  
- Hold remaining ISBNs for Book 2 / future formats (or commercial HC later if Plan B-full)

### Barcode
- The barcode is your **ISBN as a scannable EAN-13**.  
- Platforms can **generate** the graphic; you must still **place it** or leave a clear plate on the cover.  
- Typical retail rules (Lulu Global Dist): **black bars on white**, ~**2″ × 1.2″**, ≥ **0.25″** from trim edges.  
- Put ISBN as **plain text** on the **copyright page** (not a barcode image inside the book).  
- Keep QR / credits clear of the barcode zone (may need a small cover nudge).

## 5. Lulu’s role (honest)

**Lulu is excellent for:**
- What you already shipped (Premium Color · Matte · square HC/SC)  
- Author copies and gifts  
- **Lulu Bookstore** (high share of markup above print)  
- **Lulu Direct** → Shopify / your site (often highest $/sale)

**Lulu Global / Retail Distribution:**
- Can list eligible books toward **Amazon + Ingram** (~weeks for listings; ~$4.99 review fee; proof required)  
- Needs ISBN + matching metadata + barcode on cover + title/copyright pages  
- Works as a **simpler** “get me in retail” path  
- Royalties are thinner than selling Amazon via KDP and wholesale via Ingram yourself  

**Verdict:** Keep Lulu for Direct and quality you trust. For a serious “sell everywhere” business, **don’t make Lulu Global your only Amazon/bookstore strategy**.

## 12. Hands-off fulfillment — “they buy, it prints, you get paid”

Ranked for **least ongoing work** after setup:

### #1 Best match: Amazon KDP (paperback + Kindle)

| Step | Who does it |
|------|-------------|
| Customer hits Buy on Amazon | Amazon |
| Print + pack + ship | Amazon POD |
| Customer service / returns | Amazon |
| Money | Royalty → your **bank via EFT** |

- Setup once: account · tax interview · bank · upload · price · publish.  
- Ongoing: almost nothing (unless you run light ads).  
- Fits softcover-first (8.5² PB + fixed-layout Kindle).  
- Royalties usually pay on a ~60-day lag by marketplace (confirm in KDP help).

### #2 Easy second link: Lulu Bookstore

Customer buys on your Lulu product page → Lulu prints/ships → royalty to your Lulu payout. Share one URL. High margin; you drive traffic.

### #3 Optional brand store: Lulu Direct + Shopify

Shopify takes payment → Lulu auto-prints/ships (auto-approve + auto-pay) → you keep retail minus print/ship. More setup (Shopify fee); still automated day-to-day.

### Skip for v1 (not consumer Buy-button)

**IngramSpark** = stores/libraries order you; not Amazon-style checkout. Add later only if wholesale matters.

```
Customer → Amazon Buy → Amazon prints & ships → KDP royalty → EFT to your bank
Optional parallel: Lulu Bookstore / Shopify+Direct
```

## 15. Post-QC implementation order (start here when books arrive)

**Gate:** Physical HC + SC QC done · Jon signed off on any must-fix list · then run this sequence.

### Step 0 — QC gate (do not skip)
1. Open both books. Note color, spine, wrap, QR, paper feel, softcover binding.  
2. Write a short **must-fix / nice-to-have** list (chat or `Media/development/_qc-notes/`).  
3. Decide: print/content changes first, or commercial files can proceed in parallel.

### Step 1 — Phase 0 legal (before any ISBN spend / upload)
1. One-pager: Jack = author · Jon = illustrator / designer / publisher (or agreed titles).  
2. Revenue split + who owns imprint / bank payee.  
3. Pick imprint name.

### Step 2 — Identity
1. Bowker account → buy ISBN pack → assign paperback (+ ebook if wanted).  
2. Generate barcode graphic (Bowker / free EAN tool / KDP can help at upload — still leave cover plate).

### Step 3 — Commercial files (InDesign)
1. Duplicate gift INDD → **retail** project (do not overwrite gift masters).  
2. Trim / rewrite personal thank-you & gift-only back-matter (§7).  
3. Add copyright page (©, imprint, ISBN text, credits).  
4. Softcover cover: barcode clear zone; keep QR/credits clear of it.  
5. Export retail interior + softcover cover PDFs → staging under `Output/` or `Media/finals/` when Lulu-grade.  
6. Build **fixed-layout Kindle** EPUB (separate from print PDF).

### Step 4 — Hands-off sell (KDP first)
1. KDP account · tax interview · bank EFT.  
2. Create paperback 8.5×8.5″ · upload · AI disclosure · price · order **KDP proof**.  
3. Approve proof → Publish paperback.  
4. Upload Kindle fixed-layout → Publish (no Select if wide ebook later).  
5. Author Central + A+ Content + keywords/categories/blurb.

### Step 5 — Optional direct
1. Lulu Bookstore softcover (Bowker ISBN if Global Dist later; free Lulu ISBN OK for Bookstore-only).  
2. Or Shopify + Lulu Direct when you want a branded Buy button.

### Step 6 — Soft launch → Christmas
1. Soft list when live.  
2. Honest review asks.  
3. Sept–Nov polish; Nov–Dec optional Sponsored Products.

**Out of scope for first sell SKU:** commercial hardcover · IngramSpark · KDP Expanded Dist + Ingram double-list · free platform ISBNs for multi-store.
