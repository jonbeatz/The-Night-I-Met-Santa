## About This Story (locked — Draft A)

**Page title:** About This Story

One Christmas Eve, a curious child hears a noise downstairs among the toys. What begins as a tiptoe peek turns into something unforgettable: a quiet hour with Santa himself — stories, laughter, hot cocoa instead of milk, and one small wish for Christmas that isn’t gifts at all.

Jack Farrell wrote *The Night I Met Santa* for his family so that wonder would never grow old. This book brings his poem to life — page by page, picture by picture — for children, parents, and grandparents who still believe a little Christmas magic can find you when you least expect it.
