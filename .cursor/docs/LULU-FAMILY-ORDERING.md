## Pricing gotcha (do not repeat)

**Fixed List Price** often shows the number but **does not save** → `pricing: null` → **Final Review stays grey**.

**Fix:** **Set Price by → Revenue Goal** → enter ~$2.03 (SC) or ~$2.98 (HC) → Tab/blur → Lulu fills all currencies (~$12 / ~$22 USD) → then Final Review works.
